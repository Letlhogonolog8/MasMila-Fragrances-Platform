MAS'MILA FRAGRANCES WEBSITE PROPOSAL 
Shopify E-Commerce Website + Reseller & Team Management Portal 
Developer Request for Quotation / Technical Specification 
Business: Mas'Mila Fragrances 
Platform preference: Shopify 
Currency: South African Rand (ZAR) 
Primary market: South Africa 
Business model: Direct-to-consumer retail + reseller sales + sales-based team leadership 
incentives 
1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE 
Mas'Mila requires a professional Shopify e-commerce website selling a range of 50ml and 
100ml fragrances, supported by a secure reseller portal. 
The system must allow Mas'Mila to: 
1. Sell fragrances directly to consumers at the recommended retail price. 
2. Register and manage approved resellers. 
3. Give resellers access to wholesale pricing. 
4. Allow resellers to place stock orders. 
5. Track reseller sales activity. 
6. Allow qualifying resellers to build sales teams. 
7. Calculate Team Leader and Manager incentives based on qualifying product sales. 
8. Track team performance and organisational volume. 
9. Automatically calculate commissions/incentives. 
10. Provide dashboards for Mas'Mila administrators, resellers, Team Leaders and 
Managers. 
11. Maintain a complete audit trail of orders, sales, qualifications and commission 
calculations. 
12. Allow compensation percentages and qualification rules to be changed by the 
Mas'Mila administrator. 
The system must be designed for future scaling to thousands of customers and hundreds of 
resellers.

15. TEAM LEADER 
Qualification: 
All of the following: 
 Minimum 5 active direct resellers 
 Minimum 20 personal bottles sold per calendar month 
 Minimum 100 qualifying team bottles per calendar month 
 Account in good standing 
The system must automatically calculate qualification. 
Team Leader incentive: 
5% 
Calculated on qualifying wholesale sales generated by the Team Leader's qualifying direct 
team. 
Example: 
100 bottles × R140 = R14,000 qualifying sales. 
5% × R14,000: 
R700 Team Leader incentive 
16. MANAGER 
Qualification: 
 Minimum 3 active Team Leaders 
 Minimum 15 active resellers in organisation 
 Minimum 300 qualifying organisational bottles/month 
 Minimum 20 personal bottles/month 
 Account in good standing 
Manager incentive: 
2% 
Calculated on qualifying sales generated by qualifying Team Leaders directly under the 
Manager, subject to the final compensation-plan rules.

18. INACTIVITY RULES 
Reseller 
If a reseller sells 0 bottles in a calendar month: 
Status: 
Inactive 
They retain their account and products already purchased. 
They do not receive leadership incentives while inactive. 
Reactivation 
Sell 10 bottles during a calendar month. 
No rejoining fee. 
19. TEAM LEADER INACTIVITY 
Team Leader must maintain: 
 20 personal qualifying bottles/month 
 100 qualifying team bottles/month 
If they fail to qualify: 
Month 1: 
Warning/coaching status. 
Month 2: 
Team Leader status becomes inactive. 
They revert to: 
Reseller 
They retain their reseller account. 
No additional joining fee. 
They can regain Team Leader status by meeting the qualification criteria again.
